Figure 3. Predictor-group importance. Cross-validated performance of intercept-only, time-only, and time-plus-predictor models, evaluated separately for DOC and nitrate relative to the intercept-only baseline within each analyte. Points show 100 × (RMSE_intercept-only − RMSE_model) / RMSE_intercept-only; positive values indicate lower held-out error and negative values indicate higher error than intercept-only. The watershed group includes watershed area, runoff, baseflow index, forest cover and soil organic matter (DOC) or clay content (nitrate). The wildfire group includes watershed burned percentage and high-severity burn percentage. Time since fire is unpenalized in all three time-plus-predictor models; added group predictors receive the LASSO penalty. Rows are ordered intercept only, time, time + wildfire, time + watershed, and time + watershed + wildfire. The intercept-only model is a hierarchically weighted training mean and is shown at zero. Outer leave-one-publication-out folds are identical across all five fitted models; group-model penalties use up to five publication-grouped inner folds, training-fold preprocessing and the one-standard-error rule. Fitting weights balance publications, pairs and available years. RMSE is the square root of the mean held-out-publication MSE, with annual records equally weighted within each held-out publication. DOC includes 35 annual responses from seven publications; nitrate includes 68 responses from 12 publications. RMSE and absolute reductions are in lnRR units. DOC: intercept-only RMSE 0.3733; time-only RMSE 0.3787 (reduction -0.0053; -1.4%); time + watershed RMSE 0.2893 (reduction 0.0840; 22.5%); time + wildfire RMSE 0.3787 (reduction -0.0053; -1.4%); time + watershed + wildfire RMSE 0.2712 (reduction 0.1021; 27.3%); intercept-only RMSE 0.3733 (reduction 0.0000; 0.0%). NO3: intercept-only RMSE 0.8666; time-only RMSE 0.7928 (reduction 0.0738; 8.5%); time + watershed RMSE 0.7928 (reduction 0.0738; 8.5%); time + wildfire RMSE 0.7928 (reduction 0.0738; 8.5%); time + watershed + wildfire RMSE 0.7928 (reduction 0.0738; 8.5%); intercept-only RMSE 0.8666 (reduction 0.0000; 0.0%). Values within numerical tolerance of zero are displayed as zero. Points summarize aggregate held-out predictive performance. Comparing each time-plus-group model with time alone identifies the additional predictive information from its group. For nitrate the four time-containing models have the same predictions within numerical tolerance; their improvement over intercept-only is attributable to time. Cross-analyte comparisons are descriptive because publications, response variation and soil predictors differ. Groups contain five watershed and two wildfire candidates. Results quantify conditional predictive contribution, not causal importance. Publications sharing a wildfire can occur on opposite sides of a validation split. These time-adjusted group models are distinct from the all-candidate-penalized selection models in Figure 4 and the omission diagnostics in SI Figure 3.
