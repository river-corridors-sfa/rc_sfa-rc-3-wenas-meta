Figure 4. Predictor-selection stability. Points show the percentage of successful publication-bootstrap refits selecting each predictor in the full eight-predictor LASSO set at the one-standard-error penalty. Separate models use equal publication–pair–available-year fitting weights, training-fold preprocessing and publication-grouped tuning. Frequencies use 994 successful DOC and 1,000 nitrate refits from 1,000 attempts per analyte; six DOC attempts had fewer than three unique publications. All candidates, including time since fire, are penalized in these full-set selection models. In contrast, Figure 3 uses dedicated group-comparison models with time unpenalized. Selection frequencies are conditional stability summaries, not significance tests or causal importance rankings. Publication-based resampling does not ensure independent wildfire settings.
