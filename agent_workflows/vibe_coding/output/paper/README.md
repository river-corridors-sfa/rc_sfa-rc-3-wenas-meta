# Paper figures and final primary LASSO

This folder contains the manuscript-facing outputs for the current reviewed-pair annual lnRR analysis. Run `N_BOOTSTRAP=1000 Rscript agent_workflows/vibe_coding/R/run_paper_workflow_agent_v1.R` from the repository root to regenerate them from the prepared model table. Set `PAPER_FROM_SOURCE=1` to rebuild the reviewed annual table first. The ordered DOC and nitrate predictor sets are in `config/issue6_revised_primary_predictors.csv`.

The SI correlation matrix includes all 20 screened candidates in `config/predictor_dictionary.csv` plus `time_since_fire`, `followup_year`, `burn_sev_mod`, and `burn_sev_low` (24 fields total). It is a descriptive pair-year matrix, so static watershed attributes repeat across years. SI Figure 2 contains only the nine distinct predictors in the two final eight-variable sets. Figure 1 adapts the human-authored `R_scripts/archive/10_Map.Rmd` for the 14 unique fires in the current annual model table (15 study/fire records across 12 studies). Rhea and Writer study different watersheds associated with High Park; their records are retained, while Wagner's redundant observations are excluded. Figure 1 shows High Park once at the local fire-boundary centroid, with its three included burned watersheds counted together. Original `Map_input.csv` coordinates are used where available, with study metadata for other missing entries. The map keeps the original North America layout, equal area projection, fire color palette, and circle sizes for burned watershed counts. U.S. states come from the repository's Census shapefile and Canadian provinces from the local [Natural Earth vector data](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector) copy in `data/source/`. The study/fire detail behind the 14 map rows is in `tables/figure_1_study_fire_records.csv`.

Figure 2 shows every finite annual watershed-pair response, unweighted. Points are pair-years, boxes show the median and interquartile range, whiskers extend to 1.5 times the interquartile range, and the dashed line marks zero. Observation and study counts remain below each analyte. Manuscript figure titles, subtitles, and explanatory notes belong in the manuscript captions rather than inside the figures.

Figure 3 shows predictor selection only, ranking each analyte's predictors by the percentage of 1,000 study bootstrap refits in which they were selected at `lambda.1se`. Nitrate precedes DOC, and their blue and ochre colors match Figure 2. Leave-one-study-out predictions and RMSE remain available in `tables/heldout_predictions.csv` and `tables/performance.csv`; they are omitted from the figure because the displayed RMSE pooled pair-year rows rather than weighting studies equally. The current effect-size variance and shared-reference treatment remain provisional. The former issue #6 curated runs and older figures are preserved under `agent_workflows/vibe_coding/archive/preliminary_2026_09_29/`.

Annual effect sizes use `log(annual_mean_burn / annual_mean_reference)`, with arithmetic means over the same matched positive-concentration dates within calendar year. `lnRR_mean` retains this response for compatibility. Paired delta variances include same-date covariance but remain provisional with respect to temporal dependence and interpolation; singleton variances remain missing. The comparison with mean daily log ratios is in `data/audit/annual_estimand_comparison.csv` (relative to the workflow root).
