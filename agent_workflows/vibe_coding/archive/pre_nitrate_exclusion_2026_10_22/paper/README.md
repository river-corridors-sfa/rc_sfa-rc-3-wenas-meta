## Effect of the selected balanced synthesis on figures

Author decision, October 22, 2026: the primary synthesis is the equal-study → equal-pair → equal-available-year pooled log ratio, with connected study/fire-component CR2 95% intervals and Satterthwaite degrees of freedom. Balance determines the estimate; clustering determines its uncertainty. Studies retain equal weight even when two publications share one dependence component. Primary pooled results are in `balanced_synthesis/balanced_CR2_results.csv` (`status = primary_synthesis`); weights, cluster membership, deletion diagnostics and session information are alongside it. Publication-cluster and observed-only/matched-interpolated results are sensitivities; temporal fits are exploratory. REML outputs remain conditional sensitivities using provisional annual variances.

| Figure | Consequence of this decision |
|---|---|
| **Figure 1 — maps** | Keep the 14 fire markers, 15 study/fire records, 12 publications and watershed-count symbol sizes. Geographic coverage is not a weighting display. Explain in the caption that Rhea and Writer share the High Park dependence component: inference has six DOC and eleven nitrate components, rather than seven and twelve independent publication clusters. Shared clustering does not remove or merge their study observations. |

| **Figure 3 — LASSO selection** | Selection frequencies and the eight-predictor sets stay unchanged. Publication-based validation/bootstrap groups remain unchanged; do not call their results fire-component-validated. Frequencies are exploratory stability evidence, not pooled effects, significance tests, or proof of recovery. |
| **SI Figure 1 — correlations** | Retain its descriptive role. The existing unweighted pair-year matrix repeats static attributes and is not a balanced correlation matrix or an independent-cluster sample.
